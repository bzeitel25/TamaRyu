# 🐉 TamaRyu

Welcome to **TamaRyu**, a web-based virtual pet game where you raise and care for your very own dragon! 

🎮 **[Play TamaRyu Live Here!](https://bzeitel25.github.io/TamaRyu/)**

## 📱 How to Install on Your Phone
TamaRyu is a Progressive Web App (PWA), meaning you can install it directly to your phone's home screen for a native, fullscreen app experience!

1. Open the [live link](https://bzeitel25.github.io/TamaRyu/) in your mobile browser (Safari on iOS, Chrome on Android).
2. Tap the **Menu** (⚙️) button in the bottom navigation bar.
3. Select **"📱 Install App to Phone"**.
4. Follow your browser's prompt to add it to your home screen!

*(Note: If the button doesn't automatically trigger the prompt, you can manually select "Add to Home Screen" from your browser's share or settings menu).*

## 📖 How to Play

### 🥚 Evolution Stages
Your dragon will grow over time as long as you take good care of it!
1. **Egg**: Wait patiently for it to hatch (takes 30 seconds).
2. **Baby**: Needs lots of care and attention.
3. **Child**: Big enough to start playing more active games!
4. **Teen**: Rebellious, but can finally stretch its wings.
5. **Adult**: A fully grown, powerful dragon.

### 💖 Care & Needs
- **Feed (🥩/🍎)**: Keep your dragon's hunger bar full! Meat provides a large meal, while apples are quick snacks.
- **Clean (✨)**: Dragons make messes! If poops pile up, your dragon will get sick. Tap the clean button to sweep them away.
- **Sleep (💤)**: Your dragon uses **Energy** to play minigames. When energy runs low, tuck them into bed to recharge over time.
- **Heal (💖)**: If your dragon gets sick from poor care, give it medicine to restore its health.

### 🎮 Minigames
Playing games keeps your dragon happy and earns you **Coins (🪙)**! Each game costs **20% Energy** to play. As your dragon grows up, new games will unlock:
- **⚾ Fetch (Baby+)**: A classic game of 3D ping-pong. Tap the ball to toss it back to your dragon!
- **🍎 Sky Drop (Child+)**: Drag your dragon left and right to catch falling food and coins, but avoid the rocks!
- **🦇 Dragon Glide (Teen+)**: Tap the screen to flap your dragon's wings and navigate through the stalactites!
- **🔥 Dragon Fire (Adult)**: A slingshot-style game! Touch, pull back, and release to shoot fireballs at the targets flying overhead!

### 🛒 The Shop
Spend your hard-earned coins in the Shop! You can buy new backgrounds (like a magical forest) and cool cosmetic items to customize your dragon's nest.
